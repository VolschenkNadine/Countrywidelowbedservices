ABOUT COUNTRYWIDE LOWBED SERVICES

Countrywide Lowbed Services is a Secunda-based transport and logistics company specialising in the transportation of heavy equipment and machinery throughout South Africa.

We specialise in the transportation of heavy equipment, mining machinery, construction equipment, agricultural machinery, and abnormal loads throughout South Africa.

With over 26 years of industry experience, we provide reliable, safe, and efficient machinery moving solutions tailored to the mining, construction, agricultural, and industrial sectors.

Our experienced operations team, professional drivers, and versatile fleet ensure that every load is transported safely, on time, and in full compliance with industry standards.
